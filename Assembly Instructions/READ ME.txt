In Order to Assemble a Transmitter or Receiver You Need to Have the Following Tools, Parts, and Skills

--------------------------------------------------

SKILLS

--------------------------------------------------

Basic proficiency with a soldering iron

Basic use of heat inserts

Patience

--------------------------------------------------

TOOLS

--------------------------------------------------

Soldering Iron

Solder

Heat insert insertion tip

Curved needle nose pliers (Optional)

Some type of adhesive (either Super glue, Hot glue, or PLA glue)

Wire stripers

--------------------------------------------------

Parts

--------------------------------------------------

Transmitter or Receiver Stand

Transmitter or Receiver

Banana Jacks with nuts

18 gauge wire

Shroud

Heat Inserts

--------------------------------------------------

Notes

--------------------------------------------------

I will include a visual step by step guide in the form of txt and png files labeled in numerical order

Just a reminder that these probes are a set so you MUST have a 25Khz receiver paired with a 25Khz transmitter and the same applies to the 40Khz receivers and transmitters.

--------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT

--------------------------------------------------

WHEREVER YOU SEE A 4mm HOLE ON ANY PART, IT IS DESIGNED FOR A HEAT INSERT AND THUMB SCREW TO ALLOW YOU TO SECURE THE PART TO A RAIL/RULER.
